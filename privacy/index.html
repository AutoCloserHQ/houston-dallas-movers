Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HoustonDallasMovers.com may collect, use, and share information when you browse the site, use planning resources, or submit a moving quote request.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

HoustonDallasMovers.com is a Texas moving and relocation planning resource operated by ModelMoving. The site provides moving guides, planning tools, and moving quote request features.

When you submit a moving quote request, the information you provide may be used by ModelMoving to review your request and help connect you with selected moving partners based on route, availability, timing, and move details.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you submit a moving quote request or otherwise provide information through the site, we may collect details such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Moving-from location
  • Moving-to location
  • Estimated move date
  • Move size
  • Notes about packing, access, storage, specialty items, timing, or other move details

Website and attribution information

The site may also collect or record technical and attribution information associated with visits and quote requests, including:

  • Pages visited and page paths
  • Referring website or source
  • UTM source, medium, and campaign values when present
  • General browser, device, and interaction information made available through website analytics
  • Form-start, form-submission, and selected outbound-link interaction events

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through HoustonDallasMovers.com may be used to:

  • Process and review moving quote requests
  • Understand your route, timing, move size, and service needs
  • Help match requests with selected moving partners
  • Contact you about a moving quote request
  • Improve moving guides, forms, navigation, and website usability
  • Understand how visitors find and use the website
  • Measure website and marketing performance
  • Maintain and protect the website and related systems

4. How Information May Be Shared

Moving quote request information may be shared with ModelMoving and selected moving partners so they can review your route, timing, availability needs, and move details and communicate with you about moving services.

Information may also be processed by service providers that support website forms, analytics, hosting, security, or other website operations.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, prevent abuse, or maintain the security of the website and related services.

5. Analytics, Cookies & Website Data

HoustonDallasMovers.com uses Google Analytics to understand website usage and measure events such as page visits, quote-form interactions, lead submissions, and selected outbound-link clicks.

Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information about your browser, device, interactions, referral source, and pages visited.

Moving quote forms are submitted through Web3Forms, which helps transmit form information for processing.

Browser settings and privacy tools may allow you to limit or manage cookies and certain analytics technologies.

6. Calls, Texts & Emails

When you submit a moving quote request, you may authorize ModelMoving and selected moving partners to contact you about that request by phone, text message, or email, as described near the form submission button.

Message and data rates may apply to text messages. Communication preferences may be managed through the instructions provided in the communication or by communicating your preference to the sender.

7. Data Retention & Security

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably useful for quote-request processing, customer communication, analytics, business records, operational needs, security, and applicable legal obligations.

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to support the security of information handled through the website and related services. Online transmission and storage systems involve inherent security risks, so users should provide only information appropriate for the moving quote or planning purpose.

8. Your Choices

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights or choices regarding certain personal information. You may also request help with information you submitted through the site, subject to identity verification and applicable requirements.

You can manage certain browser cookies and tracking settings through your browser or device controls.

9. Third-Party Websites & Services

HoustonDallasMovers.com may link to ModelMoving, MoveReady, route resources, moving partners, or other third-party websites and services. Those destinations may have their own privacy practices and policies.

10. Children's Privacy

HoustonDallasMovers.com is designed for people researching and planning household or business moves. The website and moving quote request features are intended for adults who can provide move and contact information for those purposes.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as website features, service providers, business practices, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

HoustonDallasMovers.com is operated by ModelMoving. For questions about this Privacy Policy or information submitted through the site, you can use the contact options provided through ModelMoving.
